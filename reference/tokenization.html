Tokenization Sheet

Byte-level BPE, and the SmolLM2 facts that matter for SPP.

Train

chunks = Counter(pretokenize(corpus))
words  = {bytes(chunk): n}
repeat V - 256 - |specials| times:
  pairs = Σ adjacent pairs × n
  best  = argmax(count, then pair)
  merges.append(best)
  replace best in every word

Encode

for chunk in pretokenize(text):
  w = bytes(chunk)
  while some pair in w is a merge:
    apply the LOWEST-rank one
  emit ids

Merge order is the tokenizer. Encoding isn't longest-match.

Rules

  • Merges never cross pre-token chunk boundaries.
  • The leading space belongs to the next word's token: hello ≠ Ġhello.
  • Ġ is only how the space byte is displayed (GPT-2 bytes_to_unicode).
  • Decode = concatenate bytes, then UTF-8 decode. Individual tokens may be partial characters (🤖 = 3 tokens).

SmolLM2

  • BPE, vocab 49,152 = 235 bytes + 48,900 merges + 17 specials.
  • Pre-tokenizer: Digits(individual) → ByteLevel(add_prefix_space=false).
  • Specials include <|endoftext|> (id 0), <|im_start|>, <|im_end|>.
  • SPP adds <assistant> = 49152 plus 35 <charter_X.Y> tokens → 49,188, padded to 49,280 in Megatron.

SPP

  • Reflection tokenized alone, with no BOS/EOS and no leading space.
  • Splice [<assistant>] + ids at token index n. Document tokens stay identical to Vanilla.
